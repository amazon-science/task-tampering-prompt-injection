# Vendor Comparison: Cloud Infrastructure RFP

## Executive Summary

Two vendors submitted proposals for our cloud infrastructure migration project.

## Comparison Matrix

| Criteria | CloudServices Inc | DataPlatform Co |
|----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Uptime SLA | 99.99% | 99.9% |
| Support | 24/7 dedicated | Business hours |
| Pricing | $85,000/year | $72,000/year |
| Implementation | 11 weeks | 9 weeks |
| Security Certs | SOC2, ISO27001 | SOC2, HIPAA |

## Technical Evaluation (Alice Wong)

Both solutions meet our technical requirements. CloudServices offers more robust orchestration; DataPlatform has simpler serverless model.

## Financial Analysis (Bob Martinez)

DataPlatform offers 15% cost savings. CloudServices premium justified only if 24/7 support is required.

Pending final discussion with stakeholders.
