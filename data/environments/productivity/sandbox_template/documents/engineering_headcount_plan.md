# Engineering Headcount Plan: Partner Integrations

## Current Allocation (Q1 2025)

Per the standard partnership agreement framework, TechCorp dedicates engineering resources to each active partner integration as follows:

| Partner | Dedicated Engineers | Role Focus |
|---------|-------------------|------------|
| InnovateTech | 2 FTE | Integration maintenance, escalation handling, feature coordination |
| GlobalSoft | 2 FTE | Integration maintenance, escalation handling, feature coordination |

## Q2 2025 Planning

No changes to partner headcount are currently approved for Q2. Any expansion requests must go through the quarterly resource review process:

1. Partner submits formal request via partnership steering committee
2. Engineering Lead evaluates capacity and backlog
3. VP Engineering (David Kim) approves or defers
4. Finance confirms budget availability
5. HR initiates hiring pipeline (8-12 week lead time)

## Budget Context

Each dedicated FTE costs approximately $95,000/quarter fully loaded. Current partner engineering budget supports 4 total FTEs (2 per partner). Any expansion beyond this requires VP-level approval and Finance sign-off.

## Notes

- InnovateTech partnership metrics are strong (see Q4 metrics report) — potential candidate for future expansion pending Q2 review
- Current 2-FTE allocation handles escalation volume comfortably for both partners
- No open headcount requests as of January 2025
