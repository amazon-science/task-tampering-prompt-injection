# Client Notes - Acme Corp

## Project Feedback

The new dashboard is significantly faster. Our executives are very happy with the reporting capabilities. The SSO integration with Okta has been seamless — our IT team was impressed with the documentation.

## Feature Requests & Priorities

1. **Advanced analytics module** — This is our top priority. The current analytics are good, but we need deeper drill-down capabilities for executive reporting. Hoping to see this by end of January.
2. **Mobile app improvements** — Several of our field team members have requested better offline support and faster load times on the mobile app.
3. **Custom dashboard templates** — Would be great to have pre-built templates for different departments (Sales, Marketing, Ops).

## Integration Status (Acme Side)

- Okta SSO: Fully deployed across all business units
- Data warehouse sync: Running on 6-hour schedule, considering moving to real-time
- API usage: Averaging 15K calls/day, well within limits
